# Surface brief: StudyFit home + interview chat

Scope: landing page (`/`, Persuade) and interview chat (`/chats/:id`, Operate, inherits the world).
Audience: university students before exams. Action: start the interview. Proof: the mechanism itself, shown with labelled example data (no testimonials or numbers exist).
Constraints: keep concept, copy intent and features; premium and smooth but simple; one obvious action per screen; plain CSS, no Bootstrap.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your plan is filed from your own answers. The page is a library card catalogue: each answer is a typed index card, and each plan line cites the cards it came from. Refuses the split SaaS hero with a floating app mockup and three equal step cards.
OWN-WORLD: Reading-room green drawer ground (#10261E family), cool ivory card stock with a red header rule and pale blue ruling, a brass label plate for the wordmark, a red primary action. Archivo for engraved-label headlines, Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading, Courier Prime only for typed card labels and entries.
STORY: The visitor gets that StudyFit interviews you and builds a plan you can trace back to your own answers, trusts that it uses real methods, and starts the interview.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: plate wordmark, a large headline, one line of lead, a red "Start my interview" button with "10 questions · about 5 minutes". Right, at about half the width: a drawer of fanned answer cards behind a plan card whose lines carry origin tickets; hovering a ticket lifts its source card.
